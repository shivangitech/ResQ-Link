ResQ-Link Emergency Network ⚡

ResQ-Link is an intelligent emergency response network designed to bridge communication gaps during critical disasters. It connects trapped citizens with first responders in real-time, providing live GPS tracking, incident photo sharing, and automated AI triage evaluation.
